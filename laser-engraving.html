What I can Engrave

Whether you've got a clear idea or just a sketch in your head, I can engrave a wide range of materials and custom pieces. Everything is done in the shop here in Chapin, SC—no outsourcing, no shortcuts.

List of Common Engravings:

  • Wood plaques or displays
  • Challenge coins and medallions
  • Glassware (pint glasses, whiskey glasses, bottles)
  • Custom patches and coasters
  • Leather (wallets, patches, portfolios)
  • Barrel heads and signage
  • Logos and insignia
  • Personalized gifts for promotions, retirement, or special events

If it can be engraved cleanly and safely, I'll make it happen.

Materials That Take an Engrave Well

Here are some materials that work best for clean, crisp results.

Wood

Walnut, oak, maple, cherry, cedar, and more. Engraving can be light, darkened, or filled depending on the look you want.

Glass

Frosted-style engraving—great for barware and gifts.

Leather

Full-grain leather engraves beautifully. Works well for patches, belts, journals, and custom accents.

Acrylic

Sharp, modern edges. Great for logos, awards, signage, and display pieces.

Metal

Powder-coated bottles, flasks, and similar items engrave cleanly. Raw steel/aluminum have limitations—we'll talk it through.

A Quick Note on Prep Work

Some designs drop straight into the laser. Others don't.

If your artwork is low-resolution, full of tiny details, or pulled from a screenshot, I may need to rebuild or trace it so the engraving comes out crisp and centered—not fuzzy or blown out.

This isn't “design work.”
It's making sure your final piece looks the way it should.

I’ll always explain what's needed, and you approve it before I start.
