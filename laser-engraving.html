Materials That Take an Engrave Well

Here are some materials that work best for clean, crisp results.

Wood

Walnut, oak, maple, cherry, cedar, and more. Engraving can be light, darkened, or filled depending on the look you want.

Glass

Frosted-style engraving—great for barware and gifts.

Leather

Full-grain leather engraves beautifully. Works well for patches, belts, journals, and custom accents.

Acrylic

Sharp, modern edges. Great for logos, awards, signage, and display pieces.

Metal

Powder-coated bottles, flasks, and similar items engrave cleanly. Raw steel and aluminum require specialized treatment—consultation required.

The Technical Standard

  • Art Restoration: Low-resolution logos produce blurry results. We rebuilt your artwork into clean, vector-based paths to ensure a sharp, professional engrave.
  • Calibrated Execution: Every surface—from walnut to powder-coated steel—receives a custom-callibrated pass to ensure consistent depth and zero burn marks.
  • Integrated Design: We use specialized jigs and digital positioning to ensure your design is centered and scaled to the dimensions of the piece.
