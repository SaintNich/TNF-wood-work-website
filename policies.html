The Fine Print (Without the Fuss)

Every piece from TNF Wood Work is built with care, one at a time. Here's everything you need to know before placing your order - no legal jargon, just straight talk.

My Pricing Philosophy

I don't run sales, and I don't do coupon codes. Every piece I make is priced fairly - based on the cost of wood, the tools I use, the hours I put in, and the pride I take in my work.

No inflated margins. Nor artificial deadlines. Just craftsmanship with integrity. That's how I operate, and I stand by it.

Made to Order

Unless a listing is clearly marked "READY TO SHIP", all TNF Wood Work items are made to order. That means I don't keep stock sitting on the shelves - each piece is crafted after your order is placed, with care and precision.

Most orders are completed and shipped within 21 calendar days. If your project requires highly customized work or specialty materials, it may take longer - and I'll always keep you informed along the way.

Returns & Exchanges

Because everything is handcrafted - often with custom touches - I can't offer general returns.

That said:

  • If there's a quality issue or mistake on my part, I'll make it right.
  • For non-custom items, contact me within 7 days of delivery.
  • Return shipping must happen within 14 days, and items should be in original condition. Buyer pays return shipping unless the item was damaged or incorrect.

Custom Orders

Custom work is a big part of what I do - and I love it. Whether it's a personalized gift or a full build, I'll collaborate with you to get it right.

  • A down payment of 50% OR the cost of materials, whichever is greater, is required up front. This locks in your build slot and allows me to begin sourcing what's needed. The remaining balance is due before delivery or shipment.
  • Custom orders are final sale, unless I've made a mistake or something arrives damaged.
  • I'll send updates and confirm details with you before production begins.

Shipping & Lead Times

Orders ship via USPS or UPS - whichever makes the most sense based on size and location. You'll get a tracking number when your order goes out.

If your item arrives damaged or goes missing, email me at josh@tnfwoodwork.com within 7 days, and I'll work with you to fix it.

Privacy Policy

I only collect what I need to complete your order or answer your message: your name, address, and contact details.

Your payment info is processed securely through Square, PayPal, or other trusted platforms - I never see or store your card info.

I never sell or share your information. Period.

Payment Info

I accept:

  • All major credit cards
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
  • CashApp
  • After Pay

For large or specialty projects, I may offer deposit-based payment plans on a case-by-case basis.

Questions?

Got a custom idea or need clarification before you buy? I'm here to help.
