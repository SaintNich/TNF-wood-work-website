Shop Policies

Every piece from TNF Wood Work is built with care, one at a time. Here's what to expect - clear terms, no gimmicks.

Pricing Philosophy

No sales. No coupon games. Prices reflect the wood, hardware, time, and skill it takes to do it right.

Fair, transparent, and the same for everyone.

Made to Order

Unless a listing states READY TO SHIP, I build after you order.

Most orders are completed and shipped within 21 calendar days. Complex builds or specialty materials can take longer - I'll keep you posted.

Returns & Exchanges

Custom pieces can't be returned.

For non-custom items:

  • Contact me within 7 days of delivery.
  • Ship it back within 14 days in original condition.
  • Buyer pays return shipping unless I made a mistake or it arrived damaged.

If I mess something up, I'll make it right.

Custom Orders

  • Deposit: 50% or the cost of materials (whichever is greater) to reserve your slot.
  • Balance due before pickup or shipment.
  • Custom orders are final sale unless I made an error or it arrives damaged.
  • I'll confirm details (dimensions, wood, engraving) before I start.

Changes & Cancellations

Need to change something? Reach out as soon as possible. Once I've purchased materials or started the build, changes may affect price and timeline.

Shipping & Lead Times

I ship via USPS or UPS and send tracking when your order goes out. U.S. shipping only. Local pickup/delivery available around Chapin, SC.

If your package is damaged or goes missing, email at josh@tnfwoodwork.com within 7 days, and I'll help sort it out.

Privacy Policy

I only collect what I need to reply or fulfill your order - your name, contact info, and shipping address.

Payments are handled securely by Square/PayPal/other providers - I don't see or store card numbers.

I don't sell or share your information. Ever.

Payment Info

I accept payment from all of the following:

  • All major credit cards
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
  • PayPal
  • Cash App
  • Venmo
  • Afterpay

For larger builds, I can do deposit-based plans case by case.

Questions?

Got an idea or need clarification before you buy? I'm here to help.

josh@tnfwoodwork.com
