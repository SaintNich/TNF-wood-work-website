Start Your Engraving

Whether it's a tumbler, plaque, patch, or a one-off gift, this is where we get the details sorted so I can engrave it clean and sharp.

Whether you've got a finished design or just a general direction, this is the place to start. Tell me what you want engraved, and I'll follow up with the best way to make it happen—materials, prep, sizing, and anything else you need to know.

What to Include

In your message, it would help to include:

  • What you want engraved (tumbler, coaster set, plaque, glassware, etc.)
  • Whether you're providing the item or want me to supply it
  • Your artwork or logo (upload or describe what you have)
  • Size or location of the engraving
  • Any deadlines—promotions, retirements, events, etc.

Don't worry if your artwork isn't perfect. I'll take a look and let you know if it needs any cleanup before engraving.

Use the form to send everything over.

How It Works

  1. You send the idea and the artwork (or the closest thing you've got).
  2. I review the material, design, and prep needs.
  3. You get a clear quote—engraving, prep (if any), and shipping/pickup options.
  4. Once approved, I engrave it clean, centered, and consistent.

Straightforward communication—no pressure, no surprises.
