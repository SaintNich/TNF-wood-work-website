Shipping & Returns (Click to Expand)

Shipping

Unless a listing is clearly marked "READY TO SHIP!", all TNF Wood Work items are made to order. That means I don't keep stock on the shelves - each piece is crafted once you place your order.

Most orders are built and shipped within 21 calendar days from the order date.

Highly customized builds (unique sizing, detailed engraving, special materials, etc.) may take longer, and I'll always keep you updated if that's the case.

Orders are shipped via USPS or UPS, depending on size and destination. You'll receive a tracking number as soon as your order hits the road.

If your item arrives damaged or goes missing, please reach out to josh@tnfwoodwork.com within 7 days of delivery. I'll work with you to fix the issue.

I currently only accept orders within the continental United States.

Returns

Because every item is handmade - often with custom details - I can't accept general returns.

Here's how it works:

  • Custom Items: Final sale. If there's a problem with the quality or I made a mistake, I'll make it right.
  • Non-custom Items: If it's not personalized and you need to return it, contact me at josh@tnfwoodwork.com within 7 days of delivery. You'll need to ship it back within 14 days in its original condition. Buyer pays shipping unless I sent the wrong item or it arrived damaged.
