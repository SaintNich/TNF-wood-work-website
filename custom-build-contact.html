What to Include

In your message, it would help to include:

  • A short description of what you need (furniture, display, engraved piece, etc.)
  • Size or dimensions (if you know them)
  • Any must-haves or reference photos
  • Timeline or deadline (if there is one)

Don't worry if you don't know exactly what you're looking for. I'm here to help you piece it together.

How It Works
